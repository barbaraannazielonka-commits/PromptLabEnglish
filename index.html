About PromptLab

PromptLab is a free collection of more than 270 AI prompts written for English teachers in upper secondary school. It brings everything into one searchable place: lesson planning, feedback and assessment, reading and writing, differentiation and UDL, accessibility, games, vocational English, and the less visible parts of the job such as administration, difficult conversations and teacher wellbeing.

Many of these prompts also appear on my main website, AI Advisory Boards, but I wanted them gathered in one place that is quick to browse. You can search or filter by category, fill in the bracketed details, and copy a finished prompt into the AI tool of your choice.

How it can help other English educators

It saves timeA good prompt is a starting point, not a blank page.
It is practicalEach prompt tells the AI what role to play, what to ask you first, what rules to follow and how to format the answer.
It fits our contextThe focus is on the Norwegian curriculum (LK20), vocational programmes and inclusive teaching.

AI can draft, but the professional judgement stays with the teacher. Please read and adapt everything before you use it with students, and never enter student names or personal data.
